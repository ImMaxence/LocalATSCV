# Accessibility statement

Last updated: November 2, 2026.

LocalATSCV aims to let everyone build their resume, however they use a computer or a phone: with a keyboard, a screen reader, strong zoom or an adapted font.

## Compliance status

LocalATSCV is a free, personal website and is not subject to a legal accessibility obligation. It nevertheless targets level AA of the international WCAG 2.2 guidelines. No independent audit has been carried out yet, so the site cannot claim formal compliance.

## What is in place

- **Keyboard navigation**: every menu, button and dialog works without a mouse. In the template gallery, the arrow keys move between templates and Enter picks one. Every block you can drag also offers "Move up" and "Move down" in its menu.
- **Screen readers**: named buttons and fields, a clear heading structure, the language of every page declared, and announcements for important changes.
- **Display settings** (accessibility button at the top of every page): larger text, a highly legible font (Atkinson Hyperlegible, designed for people with low vision), wider text spacing, stronger contrast and reduced motion. They are saved on your device.
- **Light and dark themes**, and your system's "reduce motion" setting is respected.
- **Read aloud** for your resume and cover letter, using your browser's speech synthesis: nothing is sent over the Internet.
- **Step by step mode** in the editor: one section at a time, with a tip in plain language.
- **Resumes that recruiters can read**: every template prints real text, in reading order, with the document language declared. The gallery's "Very readable" filter lists the single-column templates with large type and strong contrast.

## Known limitations

- The resume preview in the editor is a scaled-down picture of the final document: to read it comfortably, use read aloud or your browser's zoom.
- The highly legible font covers Latin alphabets. For other scripts (Arabic, Chinese, Cyrillic…), your system font is used.
- Testing was done with automated tools (axe-core) and with the keyboard. Full testing with the NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver screen readers is still to be done.

## Report a problem

If something blocks you, write to [maxence.bonnici@outlook.fr](mailto:maxence.bonnici@outlook.fr) or open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/ImMaxence/LocalATSCV/issues). Tell us the page, what you were trying to do and, if you can, your browser and assistive technology. Every report is read and handled first.
